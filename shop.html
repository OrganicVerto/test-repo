Common Questions

Shop FAQ

Does Loop & Lark Crochet ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide. Processing and shipping times vary depending on whether an item is in stock or made to order.

Can I request a custom crochet order?

Yes. Custom orders are welcome for colors, sizes, or original designs — reach out through the contact page to discuss a commission.

What materials are Loop & Lark Crochet items made from?

Items are made with quality natural-fiber and blended yarns, chosen for softness, durability, and comfort.

How do I care for a handmade crochet item?

Most pieces should be hand-washed in cool water and laid flat to dry to preserve their shape and stitching.
